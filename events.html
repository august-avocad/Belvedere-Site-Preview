A Primier West Michigan Venue

Often regarded as "Mini Versailles", The Belvedere Inn offers an unforgettable setting for couples to exchange their vows. Every celebration is uniquely tailored to reflect your vision and style.

Personalized Planning

The Belvedere Inn is your complete wedding destination featuring elegant grounds, exquisite lodging, and gourmet dining. Our Special Events Coordinator will work closely with you from day one, handling rentals, setup, and every detail so you can focus on the joy of your day.
This hands-on, full-service approach is especially appreciated by couples planning from afar or those who prefer to leave the stress of logistics to the experts. It's one of the many things that sets The Belvedere Inn apart from other venues.

Lodging

The inn's 10 guest rooms are included in the wedding contract and have a minimum stay of two nights on weekends, three nights on holidays and weekends in July and August. Lodging costs are billed to the wedding couple, but guests may pay directly upon check-in. We also partner with nearby accommodations and can help arrange rooms for additional guests.

Indoor & Outdoor Options

We are pleased to offer outdoor events June-September with a capacity of 100-350 guests and indoor events with a strict capacity of 100 guests. We understand that guest counts can change, so if your estimated count is near the indoor limit, we highly recommend booking our lovely outdoor option.

Vendors

The Belvedere Inn provides all food and beverages through its on-site restaurant and arranges all rentals. Couples are invited to select their own florists, photographers, musicians, and officiants and will receive a list of recommended local vendors to help bring their vision to life.

Private Parties at The Belvedere Inn

Celebrate life's special moments in an elegant and intimate setting. Whether it's a birthday, anniversary, shower, rehearsal dinner, retirement party, or holiday gathering, The Belvedere Inn offers a refined dining experience designed to make your occasion unforgettable.

Spaces & Capacity

Private parties are hosted in the restaurant dining rooms and can accommodate up to 40 guests. Parties of 20 or more are guaranteed a private room. Smaller parties may be seated in the main dining room or may reserve a private room for an additional fee.

Dining Experience

Each event includes two hours of dining, with the option to add an additional hour for a cocktail reception. All food and wine selections are pre-chosen from the seasonal restaurant menu, ensuring every guest enjoys the best of what our kitchen has to offer.

Atmosphere & Amenities

Private parties at The Belvedere Inn focus on exceptional food, service, and ambiance. For events that include entertainment, dancing, photography, or ceremonies, we recommend exploring our Weddings and Corporate Events sections.

Scheduling & Pricing

Private parties can be booked during regular restaurant hours or arranged as a special private opening outside normal hours for a more exclusive celebration.
Event pricing is based on a minimum food and beverage total which reflects the time of year and party size.


For questions, pricing estimates, or to schedule a visit, contact us here.

Think Beyond the Boardroom

The Belvedere Inn combines the professionalism your team needs with the inspiration of a refined, private setting—perfect for corporate meetings, retreats, and executive gatherings.

Meeting Spaces

Our private meeting room accomodates
  • up to 16 guests seated around a central table
  • up to 25 guests seated at tables facing a presenter
  • up to 40 guests seated theater-style for presentations or talks
For larger groups, exclusive facility rentals are available for up to 100 guests.
Our sunroom, library, and terrace offer inviting spaces for breakout sessions or informal discussions.

Amenities

The Belvedere provides complimentary Wi-Fi and can arrange audio-visual equipment as needed. Breakfast and lunch can be served in a separate dining room or as a working meal within the meeting space. Afternoon snacks and all-day beverage service are also available.

Lodging

Special corporate lodging rates are available Sunday through Thursday for guests attending a meeting or retreat.

Dining & Evenings

When it's time to unwind, continue conversations into the evening, or sit for an executive dinner, our on-site restaurant offers exceptional food and wines in a relaxed, elegant atmosphere.
