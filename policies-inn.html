Inn Policies

Prices and availability are subject to change and vary depending on season, occupancy, special packages, and promotions. Advertised rates may differ from quoted rates. Rates quoted at booking are guaranteed.

Minimum Stay Requirements

Inn reservations require a minimum stay of two nights on weekends, three nights on holidays and on weekends in July and August. Minimum stay requirements may be reduced at the innkeeper's discretion when availability permits. If you are interested in a stay that falls below the standard minimum, please contact us to inquire about available options.

Room Requests

Every effort is made to honor the room you select at booking. In the rare event that a room change is required, you will be placed in a comparable or upgraded room at no additional cost. The Belvedere Suite, as our signature suite, is always guaranteed as booked.

Deposits

A deposit of 50% of the total room cost is required at booking to secure your reservation. All add-ons and retail items, including packages, enhancements, and in-room amenities, are charged in full at the time of purchase. For guests using a gift certificate, all applicable charges are drawn from the certificate first. A valid credit card is required to secure every reservation, even when a gift certificate covers the full cost. Any remaining balance is charged automatically on the day of arrival to the credit card provided at booking. Should you need to change your credit card on file, please inform us before your date of arrival. Administrative fees apply to card refunds.

Cancellations and Modifications

We understand that unforeseen situations may alter travel plans. As a small boutique inn, cancellations have a significant impact, and we rely on the policies outlined here. Please review them before booking, as we apply them to all reservations.

Changes or cancellations are accepted by telephone only up to 14 days prior to arrival (30 days for reservations with multiple rooms). Your deposit will be refunded less a $35 administrative fee per room, and you will receive a confirmation number for your records.

If you cancel or modify your reservation fewer than 14 days before arrival (30 days for reservations involving multiple rooms), we will make every reasonable effort to rebook the dates of your stay. If we are successful in rebooking your room(s) for the entire duration of your reservation, your deposit will be refunded less a $35 administrative fee per room. Otherwise the full cost of the original reservation will be charged.

Reservations for contracted special events are nonrefundable at the time of booking and cannot be cancelled or changed.

Late arrivals, early departures, and no-shows will incur the entire reservation cost.

Guest Access & Etiquette

For the comfort and safety of all guests please note the following:

  • Check-in is from 3pm-6pm and check-out is by 11am.
  • The front entrance is locked in the evening. Please ensure the door is secured behind you.
  • Breakfast is served between 9am and 10am.
  • Quoted room rates reflect double occupancy, regardless of age. Access to guest rooms and all common areas is reserved for registered guests only.
  • Guests must be 13 years or older.
  • Pets are not permitted anywhere on the property.
  • The Belvedere Inn is entirely smoke-free indoors. Outdoor smoking is allowed in the designated area. Cigars are not permitted.
  • Coolers are not permitted inside the inn, including in the guest rooms.
  • Guests are responsible for property damages caused by themselves or their visitors and agree that associated costs may be charged to the credit card on file.
  • In accordance with Michigan state law, all alcohol consumed in the common areas, including outdoor spaces, must be purchased from The Belvedere Inn.
